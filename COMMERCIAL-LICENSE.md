## Why a commercial license?

mcp-gateway is published under the **GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later**. AGPL is a strong
copyleft license: anyone may use, modify, and distribute the code (including
commercially), **but** any modified version or network-accessible service built
on it must also be released in full source form under AGPL-3.0. For many
companies — especially those building closed-source products or SaaS that they
do not want to open-source — that obligation is unacceptable.

This commercial license offers an alternative: **pay a fee to use mcp-gateway
without the AGPL copyleft obligations**, in a closed-source product.

## What the commercial license grants (summary)

A commercial licensee receives, in plain terms:

1. **No copyleft obligation.** Licensee may use, modify, and distribute
   mcp-gateway inside closed-source products, with no requirement to release
   their own source code.
2. **No network-source obligation.** Licensee may run a modified version as a
   network service without offering the source to users (AGPL §13 does not
   apply).
3. **Perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive** right to use the version of
   mcp-gateway licensed, including bug fixes and patches released to the
   licensee.
4. **No royalty** beyond the agreed license fee (unless a royalty-based tier

## How it works legally

- The public repository remains licensed under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. That
  license is irrevocable for everyone who already received the code under it.
- You, as the sole copyright holder, additionally offer **separate proprietary
  licenses** to any party that pays. This is the standard "dual-license" model
  used by MongoDB, Redis, Sentry, and many others.
- If you ever accept external contributions, you should use a **Contributor
  License Agreement (CLA)** so you retain the right to relicense the whole
  project commercially. Without a CLA, third-party AGPL contributions can only
  be distributed under AGPL, not your commercial license.

## Important notes

- AGPL is irrevocable: people who already have the code under AGPL keep those
